# Refund Policy Excerpt

Evidence-ID: kb-refund-2026-05

Renewal refunds may be approved within 48 hours when all of the following are
true:

- the renewal was accidental,
- the request was opened by an account admin,
- no new seats were used after the renewal, and
- a support manager approves the exception.

Customer-facing replies must cite the policy source and must not promise a refund
until a support manager has approved the exception.
